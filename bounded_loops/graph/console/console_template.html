bounded-loops — local, loopback-only

LOCAL posture: this page is reachable only from this machine, and the link's token is the only thing gating it — anyone else with this URL who can also reach 127.0.0.1 on this host could approve or reject in your place. A HOSTED deployment needs real authentication, TLS, and a role-checking authorizer; this console does not provide those and is not a substitute for them.
